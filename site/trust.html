What leaves your machine

Draft for the private beta. Updated 2026-09-20.

Whisplet records, transcribes and summarises on your own computer. The complete list of hosts the application can contact is below. Each one sits behind a setting, and the shipped binaries are checked in our build for containing no other hostname.

HostWhenWhat is sentYour control
dl.whisplet.ai When you click to download a speech or language model. When you have enabled update checks. The request for the file. No account, no identifier. Model downloads happen only on your click. Update checks are off by default.
api.whisplet.ai After the application crashed and you clicked Send on the report dialog. Later: when you activate a license. A crash report: stack and module list of the crashed process, the last lines of the application log, version and hardware summary. Never audio, never transcript text. See Privacy. Every report is a separate click. Never ask again is a button on the dialog.
Microsoft Store Only when installed through the Store: the Store's own update mechanism. Whatever the Store sends for any app it manages. Store settings on your device.

Nothing else, ever. Not analytics, not telemetry, not a heartbeat.

This page sets no cookies and loads no scripts. Questions: hello@whisplet.ai
