# Security policy

Whisplet processes meeting audio on the user's machine. A vulnerability that exposes recordings, transcripts
or notes matters more to us than anything else.

## Reporting

Mail support@whisplet.ai with the subject `security`. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.
Include the Whisplet version and build id (both in Settings), what you observed, and how to reproduce it.
We confirm receipt within a few days and keep you informed until the fix ships.

## Scope

The Whisplet application and its installer, the download and update hostnames, and the website.

## Out of scope

Third-party services we do not run, and issues that require a compromised machine to begin with.
